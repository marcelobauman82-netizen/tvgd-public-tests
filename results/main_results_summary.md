This file summarizes the main validation blocks of the public TVGD test suite.

## Interpretation

The public tests do not prove TVGD as a final theory. They show that the effective vacuum-response framework is not immediately ruled out by the main galactic, lensing, cosmological and covariant consistency checks.

## Important caution

Exploratory sectors such as quasar alignments, Pantheon anisotropies, JWST/COSMOS-Web and structural-time cluster tests are not part of the main validation chain yet.
